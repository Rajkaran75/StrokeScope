How judging works now. Pose is primary: a stroke counts when the tracked player’s wrist spikes in velocity while the elbow sweeps through an arc inside a ~300–500 ms window — no tap needed, and arms belonging to anyone else never count. Audio + motion coincidence stays as supporting evidence (a sound spike alone or motion alone is still ignored there). Manual Mark is ground truth and is never removed. Every clip keeps 1.5 seconds before and 0.75 seconds after contact; manual taps snap to the strongest sound spike in the prior 600 ms, falling back to a 350 ms back-date, and all sources dedupe inside 500 ms. Pose runs fully on-device — no video or pose data leaves this phone.

Auto-follow framing. Capture starts wide so lateral footwork stays in picture. Once a player is tracked, a smoothed digital crop follows their skeleton and keeps them centered — a deadband ignores small sways so the picture does not jitter, and nobody needs to re-aim the phone. If tracking is lost for over 1.5 seconds, framing falls back to the full frame. The follow state (following / full-frame) is recorded in the session log.

Frame-rate guard. Effective fps shows live on the video. If it stays under 24 fps for a few seconds, capture steps down once from 720p to 480p to recover smoothness and notes it on screen. If it is still under 24 fps at 480p, you will see a low-light hint — add light rather than raising resolution.
